Start by typing on this page. This is a real Alkuin document — notes and suggestions already live in the margin.

Write in the center. Notes and suggestions land in the margin. Nothing changes your words until you Accept.

## Ask Alkuin

Ask Alkuin to shorten a passage, make it clearer, or try another wording. Its suggestions are yours to Accept or Reject.

Highlight a passage, open **Note**, then choose **Ask Alkuin**. To leave your own comment instead, choose **Leave note**. Alkuin chooses the model for you.

## Invite and tag

Find **Invite someone** at the bottom of the document, beside **Keep on this Mac only**. Each invite is for one document. The button is hidden while that switch is on and for people invited to the document.

When they accept, `@` them in a margin note when you need their input. They can **Send changes**; you Accept or Reject the same way you do Alkuin’s.

## Files and folders

Your documents are plain files in the folder you open. Choose **Open Folder…** in the folder switcher to work in another folder. Use a folder’s menu to create a **New File** or **New Folder**. Use a file’s menu to rename, duplicate, or delete it. Choose **Reveal in Finder** to find it on your Mac.

You can close Tutorial anytime. It won’t come back every time you open Alkuin.
