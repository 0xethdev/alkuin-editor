# Tutorial

Start by typing on this page. This is a real Alkuin document — notes and suggestions already live in the margin.

Write in the center. Notes and suggestions land in the margin. Nothing changes your words until you Accept.

## Format

Highlight any words, then pick a format option. This line already mixes **bold** and *italic* so you can see the difference.

## Ask Alkuin

Highlight a passage, open **Note**, type what you want, then press **Ask Alkuin**. Read the suggestion in the margin — **Accept** or **Reject**.

Ask Alkuin picks the model for the job. You never open a menu.

To leave a note without asking, type in the composer and press **Leave note**.

## Invite and tag

Invite someone by email with **Invite someone**. When they accept, `@` them in a margin note when you need their input.

They can **Send changes**. You Accept or Reject their edits the same way you do Alkuin’s.

You can close Tutorial anytime. It won’t come back every time you open Alkuin.
