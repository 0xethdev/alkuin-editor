# Tutorial

This is a real Alkuin document. The open notes and suggestions in the margin are part of the lesson — try Accept or Reject on them.

Write in the center. Notes and suggestions land in the margin. Nothing changes your words until you Accept.

## Ask Alkuin

1. Highlight a sentence or short passage.
2. Format it if you want — bold, heading, list, or whatever fits.
3. Open **Note** so the composer appears in the margin.
4. Type what you want, then press **Ask Alkuin**.
5. Read the suggestion in the margin. **Accept** or **Reject**.

Ask for anything that belongs on the page. A few examples:

- Make this paragraph clearer
- Add a chart that shows this comparison
- Find scientific data to back this up
- Tighten this to two sentences
- Turn these notes into a short outline

Ask Alkuin picks the model for the job. You never open a menu.

To leave a note without asking Alkuin, type in the composer and press **Leave note**.

## Invite and tag

Invite someone by email with **Invite someone**. When they accept, `@` them in a margin note when you need their input.

They can **Send changes**. You Accept or Reject their edits the same way you do Alkuin’s.

## Practice

Highlight this sentence, open **Note**, ask Alkuin to make it clearer, then Accept or Reject the suggestion in the margin.

You can close Tutorial anytime. It won’t come back every time you open Alkuin.
