# Tutorial

Hey there! Thank you for choosing Alkuin. To get you started we have put together this document to explain the key features of this editor.

This is a real document in Alkuin and, just as any text in this editor, it is front and center to how we work. Feel free to start typing here to get a feel for it, or just wait until you spin up your first own document later on.

Suggestions, notes and comments stay in the margin and won't impact your text until you hit Accept.

## Format

Formatting text is **easy**, simply *highlight* the text passage you want to format and select a `formatting option` from the menu. We have seen many formats already, including the titles above as well as bold, italics and code snippets. Try it out yourself: formatted text.

## Ask Alkuin

Asking Alkuin for suggestions is just as easy, simply highlight the relevant text section and click Note. Add your note to the margin and click Ask Alkuin to get Alkuin's input. You can ask anything you like: shorten a passage, make it clearer, simply proof read, or find and add supporting data to a claim. Its suggestions are yours to Accept or Reject. You can be sure that Alkuin always chooses the best model for the given task!

## Invite and tag

Inviting collaborators to documents is just as easy: simply invite them with their email using the **Invite someone** button at the bottom of the document, beside **Keep on this Mac only**. Each invite is valid for one document only, so don't worry about sharing your other docs with people who should not read them.

Once they have accepted the invitation, `@` them in a margin note when you need their input. They can **Send changes**; you Accept or Reject the same way you do Alkuin’s.

If you wish to keep a document completely local without it being synced to Alkuin, you can simply switch on **Keep on this Mac only** and the only version of the document to ever exist will be saved locally on your Mac. These documents won't be shareable with others.

## Files and folders

Your documents are plain files in the folder you open. Choose **Open Folder…** in the folder switcher to work in another folder. Use a folder’s menu to create a **New File** or **New Folder**. Use a file’s menu to rename, duplicate, or delete it. Choose **Reveal in Finder** to find it on your Mac.

You can close Tutorial anytime. It won’t come back every time you open Alkuin.
