## Ask Alkuin

Highlight a passage, open **Note**, then press **Ask Alkuin** or **Leave note**. Ask Alkuin picks the model — you never open a menu.

## Invite and tag

Invite someone by email with **Invite someone**. When they accept, `@` them in a margin note when you need their input. They can **Send changes**; you Accept or Reject the same way you do Alkuin’s.

You can close Tutorial anytime. It won’t come back every time you open Alkuin.
